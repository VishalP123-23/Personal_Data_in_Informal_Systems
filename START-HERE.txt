Personal Data in Informal Systems — local hackathon demo

Project folder: D:\Personal Data in Informal Systems
Website: http://127.0.0.1:8000

To restart: double-click Start Demo.cmd in the project folder and keep its terminal open.
To edit: open the project folder in VS Code.
Choose a demo role on the sign-in screen, then click Sign in.

Includes React/TypeScript frontend, FastAPI backend, local PostgreSQL, patient and staff roles, appointments, released records, consent choices, rights requests, complaints, incidents, retention register, and auditing.

Verified: frontend build; 2 frontend tests; 8 backend tests; migrations and synthetic seed on PostgreSQL; all five demo logins and role endpoints; patient dashboard and records in browser.

Use synthetic data only. This is a hackathon prototype, not a production or certified compliance system. Erasure is limited to reviewed appointment deletion; retention entries require verification. No third-party messaging or cloud storage integration.
